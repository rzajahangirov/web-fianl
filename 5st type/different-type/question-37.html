Classic Homemade Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 250g All-purpose flour
  • 150g Unsalted butter (softened)
  • 100g Granulated white sugar
  • 150g Brown sugar
  • 2 Large eggs
  • 200g Dark chocolate chip
  • 1 tsp Vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Cream Butter & Sugars: In a large mixing bowl, beat the softened butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together until the mixture is light and fluffy.
  2. Add Wet Ingredients: Whisk in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract. Beat well until entirely integrated.
  3. Combine Dry Mix: Sift the flour into the wet mixture. Fold gently with a spatula until just combined, then stir in the chocolate chips. Do not overmix.
  4. Bake: Scoop rounded tablespoons of dough onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 10 to 12 minutes until the edges turn golden brown.
